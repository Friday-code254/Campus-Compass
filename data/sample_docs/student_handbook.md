# Northstar Institute Student Handbook

This fictional sample document is for demonstrating Campus Compass. It is not an official policy.

## Class attendance

Students must attend at least 75 percent of scheduled class meetings (the regular class attendance requirement) to remain eligible for the final assessment. If a medical issue prevents attendance, submit the absence form through the student portal within five calendar days after returning to class.

## Grade appeals

Students may appeal a final grade by submitting the grade review form within five business days after the grade is posted. The form should explain the specific grading concern and include relevant course work.

## Academic advising

Academic advising appointments are booked through the student portal. Walk-in advising is available on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 1:00 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. during teaching weeks. Students may visit without an appointment during those walk-in hours.
