# Northstar Institute Student Support Guide

This fictional sample document is for demonstrating Campus Compass. It is not an official policy.

## Counseling

Students can book a confidential counseling appointment through the student support portal. The institute provides up to four counseling sessions per semester at no charge. For an urgent safety concern, contact campus security or local emergency services.

## Career services

Career services offers resume reviews and interview practice. Students can book a 30-minute appointment through the careers portal. Drop-in resume reviews are held on Wednesdays from noon to 2:00 p.m.
