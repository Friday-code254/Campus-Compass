# Northstar Institute Examination Guide

This fictional sample document is for demonstrating Campus Compass. It is not an official policy.

## Missed examinations

Students who miss a scheduled examination because of illness or an emergency should submit a make-up examination request within two working days after the examination date. Supporting documentation may be requested by the examinations office.

## Examination adjustments

Requests for approved examination adjustments should be sent to the accessibility office at least five business days before the examination. Students should not send private medical records to teaching staff.
