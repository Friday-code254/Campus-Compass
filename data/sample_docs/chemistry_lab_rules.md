# Northstar Institute Chemistry Laboratory Rules

This fictional sample document is for demonstrating Campus Compass. It is not an official policy.

## Attendance requirement

Chemistry laboratory students are required to attend at least 80 percent of scheduled laboratory sessions. For lecture courses, consult the Student Handbook for its separate attendance policy.

## Safety preparation

Before the first practical session, students must complete the online lab safety checklist. Students must wear safety goggles and a buttoned laboratory coat during practical work. Food and drink are not allowed in laboratory rooms.
