# Northstar Institute Library Guide

This fictional sample document is for demonstrating Campus Compass. It is not an official policy.

## Opening hours

The main library is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. On Saturdays and Sundays it is open from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. The library is closed on institute holidays.

## Borrowing and renewals

Undergraduate students may borrow up to five books at a time. The standard loan period is 14 days. A book may be renewed twice if another student has not placed a hold on it.

## Study rooms

Study rooms can be reserved in the library portal for up to two hours per day. A reservation is released if the group is more than 15 minutes late.
